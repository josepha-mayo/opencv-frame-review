Export strategy

5× local retiming inside the detected interval only. Requires at least three repeated cycles, per-plane recurrence mean ≤2 and every sample difference ≤32 native codes. This bounded test can still admit small nonperiodic changes. Strong entry transitions remain; no safety claim.
