Before you begin

Allow about 3–4 minutes. There are no names, emails, microphones or camera recordings. The clips are silent in this study and play only when you press Play. You can pause, skip a clip or leave at any time.

Versions are called A and B. Their mapping is revealed after your answers are locked. Please take part once, work independently and do not look up answers. A small invited pilot can inform reading preference and usability; it does not establish learning improvement.

Anonymous structured answers may be summarized in a hackathon report. The app stores no IP address. Private raw records expire after 30 days; aggregate reports may remain. Keep your invitation to exclude your response from analysis.
